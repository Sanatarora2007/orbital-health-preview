Crew health checks · 2D preview

Orbital Health Checks

Two short games that preview how virtual-reality tests could track an astronaut's orientation and vision during a mission. Played here with a keyboard and mouse on a flat screen.

Lost in the Module

Measures spatial orientation: how much slower you find a hatch when you are upside down.

Blind-Spot Painter

Measures the visual field: the size and position of each eye's natural blind spot.

How to play

    Drag or arrow keys: look  ·  Q / E: roll  ·  Space or click: select

    Step 1 · Screen size

    Hold a bank card (or any card of the same size) against the screen. Move the slider until the amber box is exactly as wide as the card.
